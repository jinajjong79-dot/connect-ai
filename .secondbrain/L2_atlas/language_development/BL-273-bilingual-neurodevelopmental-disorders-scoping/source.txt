Yuen HK, Ge H, Zhang C, Wong YT, Chan EYW, Tsang WWN, Capio CM (2025). Children (Basel). PMC12468967. Scoping review (Arksey & O'Malley + JBI framework). 15 cross-sectional studies. N=982 ASD children (463 mono, 404 bi) + 644 TD (346 mono, 298 bi). Ages 5-12y.

⚠️ DATA CORRECTION: 사용자 기재 "ASD/ADHD/DLD 아동도 이중언어 불이익 없음" — 실제로는 ASD에 관한 연구만 존재. ADHD, DLD(발달언어장애), 기타 신경발달장애 연구 없음 (= 중요 근거 공백).

Key findings (ASD only):

Working memory: Bilingual ASD children → superior accuracy + faster response on 2-back tasks vs. monolingual ASD peers

Inhibitory control: Mixed results — bilingual advantage on global-local tasks, but NOT on Simon/Flanker tasks

Cognitive flexibility: Bilingual ASD children → significantly better on DCCS set-shifting tasks; occasionally matched TD peer performance

Parent-reported measures: Inconsistent with lab findings; bilingual advantages less evident in everyday EF

Conclusion: "Bilingual exposure is associated with specific cognitive benefits in children with ASD" — particularly working memory and cognitive flexibility. However:
- Research exclusively on ASD; ADHD/DLD/other = critical evidence gap
- No studies confirmed bilingual disadvantage for ASD

Key concept: "ASD 아동에서 이중언어=WM·인지유연성 이점(실험실 과제), 불이익 없음; ADHD/DLD는 데이터 없음 — '모든 신경발달장애 아동도 안전'은 근거 없는 과일반화"
