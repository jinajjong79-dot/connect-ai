# Parent & Child Perceptions of Child Health after Sibling Death

**Authors:** Rosa M Roche, Dorothy Brooten, JoAnne M Youngblut
**Journal:** International Journal of Nursing and Clinical Practice, June 2016
**PMC:** PMC5036584

---

## Abstract

Study examining perceptions of child health following sibling death, comparing parent and child self-ratings at 2 and 4 months post-death.

---

## Key Findings

### Perception Discrepancies (Parent vs Child)
- **At 2 months**: 45% of mothers' and 84% of fathers' ratings of child's Health Now were **higher** than children's self-ratings
- **At 4 months**: 54% of mothers' and 53% of fathers' ratings were higher than children's self-ratings
- **Clinical implication**: Parents systematically overestimate surviving children's health — children may be masking distress to protect parents

### Mental Health Outcomes in Bereaved Siblings
| Timepoint | Anxiety (elevated) | Depression (clinically significant) |
|-----------|-------------------|--------------------------------------|
| 2 months | **34%** | 0% |
| 4 months | 24% | 3.5% |

- Anxiety is the primary early response; decreases over time
- Depression emerges later (2 → 4 months)

### Predictors of Health Ratings
- **Child's self-rating**: Greater depression → lower health rating at 2 months
- **Father's rating**: Child's anxiety predicted lower paternal assessment
- **Mother's rating**: Chronic sibling illness (vs acute death) → more negative evaluations

### Racial/Ethnic Differences
- At 4 months: Black fathers rated surviving children's health as "better now vs before" death
- White fathers: "about the same" — statistically significant difference

---

## Study Design

- **Sample**: 64 surviving children (ages 6–17) + 48 parents, recruited from South Florida hospitals
- **Timepoints**: 2 and 4 months post-sibling death
- **Measures**:
  - Health Now scale (1–10 rating)
  - Spence Children's Anxiety Scale (SCAS)
  - Child Depression Inventory (CDI)

---

## Clinical Recommendations

1. **Interview children separately from parents** — children shield parents from emotional distress
2. **Monitor anxiety and depression** during the first post-bereavement year
3. **Coordinate with school personnel** when siblings are bereaved
4. **Consider grief support groups** and professional resources for families
5. **Do not rely solely on parental reports** — child self-report is essential

---

## Relevance to Sehyun (세현이) Care

- 형제 사별 후 **불안이 1차 반응** (34% at 2 months) → 초기 정서 지원 핵심
- 아버지가 자녀 건강을 **과대평가** 하는 경향 — 세현이의 실제 상태 직접 확인 필요
- 아동이 **부모 보호 목적으로 감정 숨김** → 별도 공간에서의 표현 기회 필요
- 만성 질병 사별(vs 급성) → 어머니 평가 더 부정적 (장기 지원 필요성)
