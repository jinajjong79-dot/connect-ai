Iwinski et al. (2020). Eating Behaviors. PMC10796209. N=110 families. Child age 18-24 months (M=21 months, SD=2.7). Longitudinal observational — home-based mealtime videotaping + caregiver surveys.

Note: Actual publication year is 2020 (user listed as 2024).

Key findings:

Correlations:
- Caregiver-reported FR ↔ observed FR: r=0.26, p=.007
- Caregiver-reported picky eating (PE) ↔ observed PE: ρ=0.21, p=.029
- Child attachment (smooth interactions) ↔ observed PE: ρ=−0.22, p=.021

Survey regression (Adj.R²=0.17, total R²=0.191):
- FR → reduced PE: β=−0.33, p≤.001
- Attachment moderates FR-PE relationship: β=−0.22, p=.015

Observational regression:
- FR → reduced PE incident rate by 34%: IR=0.660 (95% CI: 0.472–0.923)
- FR × Attachment interaction: IR=0.669, p=.024

Conclusion: Secure attachment behaviors ENHANCE the effect of responsive feeding on reducing picky eating. Relationship quality amplifies feeding effectiveness.

Key concept: "안전 애착→반응적 수유 효과 강화(β=−0.22 조절 효과): 관계 질이 좋을수록 반응적 수유→편식↓ 경로 더 강력. '관계가 좋으면 밥도 잘 먹는다' 기제 확인"
