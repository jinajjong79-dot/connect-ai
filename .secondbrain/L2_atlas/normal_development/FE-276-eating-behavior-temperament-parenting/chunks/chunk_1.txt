Zeyrek, Güney, Özaslan & Dikmen (2025). Brain and Behavior. PMC12340540. N=203 Turkish preschoolers, ages 3-6 years.

Key findings:

Parental feeding style → child eating:
- Emotional feeding → emotional overeating: r=0.316, p<.01
- Instrumental feeding → food responsiveness: B=0.22, p=.028
- Tight/coercive feeding control → food responsiveness↑: B=0.27, p=.008
- Tolerance-controlled feeding → desire to drink↓: B=−0.18, p=.032

Child temperament → eating:
- Negative affect → enjoyment of food↓: r=−0.255, p<.01 (편식 관련)
- Negative affect → satiety responsiveness↑: r=0.347, p<.01 (포만감 과반응)
- Surgency → desire to drink↑: B=0.04, p=.001
- Effortful control → emotional overeating↓: r=−0.207, p<.01

Model R²:
- Food responsiveness: 13%
- Emotional overeating: 17%
- Satiety responsiveness: 22%
- Slowness in eating: 19%

Conclusion: "Parental feeding behaviors and temperamental traits significantly influence children's eating behaviors." Tight control amplifies food responsiveness (not desired outcome). Negative affect temperament → less food enjoyment + more satiety sensitivity.

Key concept: "기질×양육 상호작용: 부정적 정서 기질↑→음식 즐거움↓(r=−.255); 강압적 수유 통제→음식 반응성↑(B=0.27); 기질 맞춤 식사 접근 필요"
