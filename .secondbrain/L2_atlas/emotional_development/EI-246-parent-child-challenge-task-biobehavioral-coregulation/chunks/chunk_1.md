=== OVERVIEW ===

This study introduced the Parent-Child Challenge Task (PCCT) to evaluate self-regulation and coregulation processes in parents and preschoolers. The researchers examined whether an experimental perturbation (time pressure) would produce observable changes across affect, behavior, and physiology domains, with concurrent and predictive validity assessed through relationships with externalizing behavior outcomes.

Key theoretical link: RSA(호흡동성부정맥) = 자율신경계 공동조절의 생리적 측정. 다미주신경이론(polyvagal theory) 연결: 기저 RSA↓ = 사회적 참여 및 미주신경 긴장 감소 → 조절 능력↓.

=== SAMPLE ===

- N=96 children (54% female; mean age=41 months, SD=3 months)
- RSA data subset: n=45 (약 47%; 무선 간섭으로 완전 데이터 제한)
- Race: 86% White, 8% Biracial
- Median household income: $65,000; 79% married
- Longitudinal: T1(41개월) + T2 follow-up(4개월 후)

=== PARENT-CHILD CHALLENGE TASK (PCCT) ===

Task design: Mothers verbally guided 3-year-olds through increasingly difficult puzzles without physical assistance.
- Baseline period: 4 minutes
- Challenge condition: 2-minute time-pressure constraint added
- Three puzzle designs of increasing difficulty

Behavioral Coding (interrater reliability 80%-100%):
- Maternal: teaching, directive statements, proactive structure, positive reinforcement, emotional support, engagement, disengagement, intrusion, negative discipline
- Child: compliance, persistence, noncompliance, disengagement, behavioral dysregulation
- Affective coding: negative / neutral / low positive / medium-high positive

RSA Measurement: Wireless ECG electrodes, frequency 0.24–1.04 Hz (children), 0.12–0.40 Hz (adults)

=== KEY FINDINGS ===

Individual Regulatory Changes (Baseline → Challenge):
- Child positive affect: ↑ (p=.032)
- Child negative affect: ↑ (p=.016)
- Maternal teaching: ↓↓ (F=26.26, p<.001, η²=.22) — 스트레스 상황에서 교육적 스캐폴딩 철수
- Maternal positive affect: ↑ (p=.003)
- Maternal intrusion: ↑ (p=.034)
- Child compliance: ↓ (p=.047)

Dyadic Regulatory Patterns:
- Dyadic affective variability: ↑ (F=10.91, p=.001, η²=.10) — 감정 불안정 증가
- Dyadic behavioral variability: ↓ (F=14.40, p<.001, η²=.13) — 행동 레퍼토리 축소
→ 감정 불안정 ↑ + 행동 유연성 ↓ 동시 발생: 스트레스 시 부모 스캐폴딩 철수가 아동 감정 조절 방해

Predictive Validity (T1 → T2 externalizing, 4-month follow-up):
1. Maternal teaching baseline↓ → 외현화↑ (B=2.88, p=.002) — FDR 보정 후 유의
2. Child positive affect change↑ → 외현화↑ (B=9.17, p=.004) — 비변별적 긍정 반응이 조절 문제 마커
3. Maternal baseline RSA↓ → 외현화↑ (B=−4.43, p<.01) — 다미주신경이론 지지: 낮은 미주신경 긴장=조절↓
4. Child RSA change(augmentation) → 외현화↑ (p=.041; FDR 후 경계)

=== SIGNIFICANCE ===

다미주신경이론(PVT) 연결 핵심:
- 모성 기저 RSA↓ → 아동 외현화 문제↑ (4개월 후 예측)
- RSA = 사회적 참여 시스템의 생리적 지표
- 낮은 미주신경 긴장 → 조절 용량 감소 → 아동 발달에 영향

공동조절 실패 경로:
시간 압박 → 어머니 교육 행동↓ → 아동 감정 불안정↑ + 행동 레퍼토리↓
= "부모가 스캐폴딩을 철수하면 아이의 조절 시스템이 무너진다"
