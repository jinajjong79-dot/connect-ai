Koss & Gunnar (2018). Journal of Child Psychology and Psychiatry. PMC5771995. Annual Research Review (literature synthesis, not single empirical study).

Study type: Annual Research Review — synthesizes findings from multiple published studies across childhood development literature.

Key synthesized findings:
- Early adversity (poverty, maltreatment, family dysfunction, parental depression) → altered HPA axis functioning
- Chronic neglect/institutional deprivation → hypocortisolism (blunted/suppressed cortisol) — not hyperactivation
- Internalizing disorders: mixed associations (elevated cortisol in some contexts, blunted in others depending on current adversity)
- Externalizing problems: more consistently associated with lower cortisol and blunted reactivity
- Secure attachment = HPA buffering (protective)
- Insecure attachment = HPA buffering failure (elevated stress response)

Pathway model: Early adversity → HPA axis dysregulation → internalizing + externalizing psychopathology

Key nuance: Type of adversity matters — threat/abuse → hyperreactivity; deprivation/neglect → hyporeactivity (HPA blunting). This distinction has implications for intervention.

Key concept: "역경 유형별 HPA 반응 분화: 위협/학대=과반응, 방임/박탈=저반응(blunting); 안전 애착=HPA 완충 vs. 불안정 애착=완충 실패"
