Bredikyte & Brandisauskiene (2023). Frontiers in Psychology. PMC10766374. N=454 children ages 3-6, 7 Lithuanian daycare centers.

Theoretical framework: Vygotskian perspective on pretend play as the leading activity for preschool development. Play creates a "zone of proximal development" for self-regulation.

Five developmental capacities in play (CP&SR Checklist):
1. Creativity
2. Motivation
3. Imagination
4. Self-regulation (voluntary behavior)
5. Perspective-taking

Key findings:
- Role adoption ↔ self-regulation: Kendall's τ=0.641, p<0.0001 (very strong association)
- 68.1% of self-regulation variance predicted by 5 play parameters + age + gender
- Pretend play quality is the strongest predictor of self-regulation among preschoolers
- Age and gender contribute but play parameters dominate the variance explanation

Measurement tool: CP&SR Checklist (Checklist for Play and Self-Regulation)

Key concept: "Pretend play = self-regulation rehearsal space (Vygotsky); role adoption is the primary self-regulation mechanism in play"
