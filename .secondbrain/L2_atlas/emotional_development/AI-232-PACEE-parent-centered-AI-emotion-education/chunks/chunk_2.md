This paper is directly relevant to the "세현이의 방" (Sehyun's Room) vision — Level 5 of the content pipeline. PACEE is the academic model for an AI system that:
- Coaches parents (not children) in real-time
- Follows Gottman's framework (same as ATLAS knowledge base)
- Builds child emotional profiles over time
- Maintains parental autonomy as primary decision-makers

PACEE = academic validation that the ATLAS + BriefingBot + content pipeline's long-term vision is feasible and evidence-based.
