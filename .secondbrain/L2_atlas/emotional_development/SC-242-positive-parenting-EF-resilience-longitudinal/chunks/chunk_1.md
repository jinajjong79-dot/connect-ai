=== OVERVIEW ===

This longitudinal investigation examined connections between executive function (EF) and parenting practices during early childhood and their effects on resilience, self-regulatory efficacy, and behavioral outcomes at age 9. The study followed 195 participants (51.3% female, median age 108 months) with measurements at ages 4, 6, and 9 years. Parents reported child EF difficulties and behavioral problems; children self-reported resilience and self-regulatory efficacy at age 9.

=== KEY FINDINGS ===

Path Analysis Results:
- Model fit: χ²(20) = 22.417, p = 0.318; CFI = 0.997; RMSEA = 0.025; NFI = 0.973 (excellent fit)
- Positive parenting at ages 4 and 6 → indirect better outcomes mediated through reduced EF difficulties
- EF difficulties at age 6 → directly predicted lower resilience at age 9
- EF difficulties at age 6 → directly predicted behavioral problems at age 9
- Positive parenting at age 4 indirectly predicted fewer EF difficulties at age 6
- Bidirectional: EF difficulties at age 4 directly negatively predicted positive parenting at age 6
- Strong correlation: self-regulatory efficacy × resilience r = 0.63 (p<0.01)

Key Bivariate Correlations:
- EF difficulties at age 6 → lower resilience at age 9: r = −0.17 (p<0.05)
- Behavioral problems at age 6 → lower resilience: r = −0.18 (p<0.05)
- Behavioral problems at age 9 → lower self-regulatory efficacy: r = −0.17 (p<0.05)

Parenting-EF relationship stronger than parenting-behavior relationship.
Age 4 associations more pronounced than age 6 (declining parental influence as children develop).

=== SAMPLE ===

- N=195 healthy 9-year-old children
- 3 measurement timepoints: ages 4, 6, and 9 years
- 51.3% female, 48.7% male; median age 108 months
- Primary caregivers: 75.6% mothers, 11.4% fathers, 10.4% grandmothers
- Median parental education: 16 years (bachelor's degree)
- Retention: 97.9% at age 4 (274 participants), 92.5% at age 6 (259 participants)
- Final N=195 = 75.3% of those followed to age 6
- 12.8% (25 participants) had developmental/behavioral problems; 18 ADHD diagnoses
- Middle to high socioeconomic status, Thailand

=== INSTRUMENTS ===

Executive Function (EF):
- Age 4: BRIEF-P (63 items), T-scores; clinically significant: T≥65
- Age 6: BRIEF (86 items), T-scores; Cronbach's α = 0.80–0.97
- Age 4: 15.3% (29/190) clinically significant EF difficulties
- Age 6: 8.0% (15/188) clinically significant EF difficulties

Positive Parenting (PSDQ 32 items):
- Authoritative − (authoritarian + permissive) = positive parenting score
- Cronbach's α: authoritative 0.86, authoritarian 0.82, permissive 0.64

Behavioral Problems:
- Age 4: CBCL 1½–5 (100 items); 18.4% above clinical cutoff
- Ages 6/9: SDQ (25 items); Age 6: 14.9% above cutoff; Age 9: 17.5% above cutoff

Resilience (Age 9):
- Resilience Factors Scale (RFS), 25 items, 4-point Likert
- Cronbach's α = 0.86; median score 78 (IQR 73, 83)

Self-Regulatory Efficacy (Age 9):
- Self-Regulatory Efficacy Questionnaire, 8 items, 5-point Likert; academic focus
- Cronbach's α = 0.80; median score 31 (IQR 28, 35)

=== SIGNIFICANCE ===

"만4세 양육이 만9세 결과를 예측": positive parenting at age 4 has cascading indirect effects through reduced EF difficulties → reduced behavioral problems → higher resilience at age 9.

Key insight: parenting acts through EF development, which then drives resilience and self-regulatory efficacy. Scaffolding+autonomy support = EF support = long-term resilience buffer.

EF difficulties at preschool → lower resilience even in low-adversity, middle/high-SES children (extends prior ACE-focused resilience research).

Bidirectional EF-parenting: child's EF problems at age 4 degrade parenting quality at age 6 — intervention in early EF may protect both child and parent-child relationship.
