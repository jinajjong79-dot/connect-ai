Expression and Co-regulation of Negative Emotion in 18-month-olds at Increased Likelihood for Autism with Diverse Developmental Outcomes
Authors: Jessie B Northrup, Kaitlyn B Cortez, Carla A Mazefsky, Jana M Iverson
Journal: Autism
Year: 2024 (Published February 26, 2024)
Source: PMC11345877
ATLAS ID: EI-247
Category: emotional_development / autism / coregulation / negative emotion / early intervention
Tier: 2 | Grade: B
Year: 2024

=== OVERVIEW ===

This study examined how 18-month-old younger siblings of autistic children (elevated likelihood group) expressed negative emotions and how parents responded. Three distinct developmental outcome groups were compared: autism diagnosis, language delay without autism, and typical development. Key finding: autistic toddlers displayed significantly more intense and persistent negative emotions; parental co-regulation strategies effectively reduced negative affect continuation by 60% across all groups.

=== SAMPLE ===

- N=39 toddlers at 18 months (23 boys, 16 girls)
- Elevated likelihood group (younger siblings of autistic children)
- 3 outcome groups:
  - ASD (autism diagnosis at 36 months): N=11
  - EL-LD (language delay, no autism): N=12
  - EL-ND (no diagnosis or delay): N=16
- Home-based video observations ~45 minutes
- Diagnosis confirmed via ADOS at 36 months

=== INSTRUMENTS ===

- ADOS (36개월): autism diagnosis
- MSEL (Mullen Scales of Early Learning): 18/24/36개월 발달평가
- CDI (MacArthur-Bates): vocabulary assessment
- Video coding system (frame-by-frame):
  - Negative affect identification: κ=0.79, 97.19% agreement
  - Negative affect intensity: ICC=0.805, 96.9% agreement
  - Co-regulation strategy presence: κ=0.74, 98.5% agreement
  - Redirection: κ=0.72, 88.4% agreement
  - Physical comfort: κ=0.77, 93.8% agreement

=== KEY FINDINGS ===

Frequency of Negative Emotion:
- Infrequent across all groups: 6-8% of 10-second intervals
- No significant group differences in frequency

Negative Emotion INTENSITY (primary finding):
- ASD group: M=0.24, SD=0.25
- EL-ND (typical): M=0.05, SD=0.09
- EL-LD (language delay): M=0.11, SD=0.15
- Kruskal-Wallis: H=5.98, p=0.05, η²=0.11
- ASD vs. typical: significantly higher intensity

Parental Co-regulation Response:
- Parents responded in ~61% of intervals containing negative affect — high responsiveness across all groups
- No significant group differences in frequency of parental responses (p>0.05)
- Solution-focused strategies: significant group difference (H=10.24, p<0.001, η²=0.23)
  - Typical development parents used more than language-delayed children's parents

PERSISTENCE ("stickiness") of Negative Affect (time-series):
- Baseline continuation odds: OR=28.41 (95% CI=18.57-43.46, p<0.001) — 한번 시작되면 계속되는 경향 강함
- ASD group greater persistence: OR=2.24 (95% CI=1.15-4.35, p=0.017)
- EL-LD group greater persistence: OR=2.01 (95% CI=1.04-3.88, p=0.038)
- Co-regulation effectiveness: OR=0.40 (95% CI=0.24-0.68, p=0.001) — 부모 공동조절 시 부정 감정 지속 60%↓

Developmental Assessment (18-month MSEL):
- ASD vs. typical: gross motor (p=0.003), visual reception (F=10.39, p<0.001), receptive language (p=0.011), expressive language (F=5.82, p=0.007)
- CDI words produced: ASD M=11.82 vs. typical M=30.67 (p=0.004)

=== SIGNIFICANCE ===

자폐 조기 발견 근거:
- 자폐 진단 전(18개월)에 이미 감정 강도↑ + 지속성↑ 관찰 가능
- 빈도가 아닌 강도와 지속성이 구분 마커

공동조절의 보편적 효과:
- 모든 집단에서 부모 공동조절 → 부정 감정 지속 60%↓
- 단, 자폐 집단에서 효과 다소 약한 경향(통계적 비유의)

언어 발달 연결 가설:
- 감정 조절 어려움 → 언어 학습 기회 방해 → EL-LD 집단도 중간 수준 지속성
- 공동조절 = 언어 발달의 선제 조건

임상 방향:
- 표준 공동조절 전략이 자폐 고위험군에서도 효과적이지만, 수정·보강 필요 가능성
- 18개월 감정 강도/지속성 측정 = 자폐 조기 선별 마커 후보
