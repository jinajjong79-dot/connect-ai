Current Effect of Mother-Child Memory Talk on Emotion Regulation, Self-Esteem, and Memory
Author: Nilsu Borhan
Institution: Pamukkale University, Department of Psychology, Denizli, Türkiye
Journal: International Journal of Assessment Tools in Education
Year: 2024 (Vol. 11, No. 1, pp. 148-170)

=== OVERVIEW ===

Retrospective study examining how the frequency of mother-child memory conversations in childhood predicts emotional regulation, self-esteem, and emotional memory content in young adulthood. N=124 young adults (ages 22-39). Path analysis reveals significant serial mediation: maternal reminiscing frequency → self-esteem → emotion regulation → emotional vocabulary.

=== SAMPLE ===

- N=124 young adults (M age=24.88, SD=3.90; range 22-39)
- 84 females (67.7%), 40 males (32.3%)
- Recruited via METU Psychology portal (convenience sampling)
- 80.6% bachelor's degree
- Eligibility: living mothers

=== INSTRUMENTS ===

Family Reminiscence Scale (FARS, 10 items, 5-point Likert):
- GRER: General Recurrent Events Reminiscence (α=.85)
- FER: First-time Events Reminiscence (α=.81)
- Example: "How often did your mother talk about any holiday experience?"

Rosenberg Self-Esteem Scale (RSES, 10 items): α=.91
Emotion Regulation Difficulty Scale-Short Form (DERS-16, 16 items): α=.94
Emotional Memory Coding:
- Participants wrote one emotionally negative recent memory with mother
- Total words, Total emotion words, Unique emotion words (vocabulary diversity)
- ICC Total words=1.00, emotion words=.981, unique words=.957

=== KEY FINDINGS ===

Model Fit: χ²(4)=6.01, TLI=.97, CFI=.99, NFI=.96, RMSEA=.06

Direct Significant Paths:
1. Maternal reminiscing frequency → Self-esteem: β=.27, p=.001 (95% CI [.12, .41])
   어릴 때 엄마와 기억 이야기를 자주 한 사람 → 성인기 자존감 높음
2. Self-esteem → Emotion regulation difficulty: β=-.55, p<.001 (95% CI [-.68, -.37])
   자존감↑ → 감정조절 어려움↓ (강한 음적 관계)
3. Emotion regulation difficulty → Emotional vocabulary:
   - Total emotion words: β=.23, p=.036
   - Unique emotion words: β=.28, p=.008
   ⚠️ 역설: 감정조절 어려움↑ → 부정 감정 기억 서술 시 더 많은 감정 단어 사용
   → 과도한 감정 표현 = 조절 어려움의 지표일 수 있음

Indirect Effects (Serial Mediation):
- Maternal reminiscing → Self-esteem → Emotion regulation → Total emotion words: -.12, p=.011
- Maternal reminiscing → Self-esteem → Emotion regulation → Unique emotion words: -.15, p=.003
- Maternal reminiscing → Self-esteem → Emotion regulation difficulty: -.15, p=.005

전체 경로: 어릴 때 엄마와 기억 대화 자주 함 → 자존감↑ → 감정조절 어려움↓ → 부정 기억 서술 시 감정 어휘 적음(=조절 잘 됨)

Non-Significant:
- Direct path: Maternal reminiscing → emotional memory content (not significant — frequency vs. quality distinction)
- Gender differences: not significant (METU sample 특성)

=== THEORETICAL MECHANISMS ===

Theory of Mind (ToM): 기억 대화에서 내부 상태(생각·감정·욕구) 논의 → 타인의 마음 이해 능력 발달 → 자기 이해 → 자존감·감정조절

Attachment Theory: 정기적 감정 조율 기억 대화 → 안정 애착 강화 → 회복탄력성·자존감·감정관리

=== ATLAS SIGNIFICANCE ===

NR-251 = 회상 대화의 장기(성인기까지) 효과 확인:
- 어릴 때의 기억 대화 빈도가 성인 자존감을 예측 (β=.27)
- 자존감 경로를 통해 성인 감정조절에 간접 영향
- 아동기 효과가 성인기까지 지속 = elaborative reminiscing의 생애발달적 효과 근거

실용적 함의:
- 특별한 훈련 없어도 일상적 기억 대화의 "빈도"만으로도 효과
- 부모가 매일 저녁식사 때 하루를 같이 돌아보는 것 = 과학적 근거 있는 감정교육
