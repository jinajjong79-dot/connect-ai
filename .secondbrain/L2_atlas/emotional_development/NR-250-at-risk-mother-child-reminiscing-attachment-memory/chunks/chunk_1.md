=== OVERVIEW ===

This study examined how maternal attachment security, emotional elaboration, and structural elaboration during mother-child reminiscing conversations jointly predict children's autobiographical memory specificity in a low-income, at-risk sample. Distinguishes two elaboration types — emotional (feelings) vs. structural (narrative scaffolding) — and shows their interaction with maternal attachment predicts child memory development.

=== SAMPLE ===

- N=95 low-income mother-child dyads
- Child age: 3.5–6 years (M=5.55, SD=0.87); 54.2% male
- Race: 60.6% African American, 21.3% Caucasian, 9.6% Latino
- 67.4% earned less than $17,000/year (high poverty)
- Maternal education: 21.1% ≤11th grade, 28.4% HS/GED, 35.8% some college
- 4 videotaped reminiscing tasks: happy event / acutely stressful event / ongoing conflict / separation experience

=== INSTRUMENTS ===

Autobiographical Memory Test — Preschool Version (AMT-PV):
- 10 cue words (5 positive, 5 negative)
- Responses coded: specific (single day) vs. overgeneral (multiple occasions/categories)
- Average: 47% specific memories; 33% "no memory" responses

Maternal Attachment (ECR-R, 36 items):
- Attachment avoidance (1-7)
- Attachment anxiety (1-7)

Maternal Elaboration Coding (ICC=.94-.99):
1. Structural elaboration: open-ended questions, yes/no questions, elaborative statements, confirmations
2. Emotional elaboration: emotion questions, positive/negative emotion attributions

=== KEY FINDINGS ===

Two-Way Interaction: Emotional × Structural Elaboration
(β=0.23, p<.05; R²=.28, p<.01 — explains 28% variance)
- High emotional + high structural → children with significantly more specific memories
- High emotional + LOW structural → WORSE memory specificity (negative prediction, b=−2.14, p<.01)
- Low emotional: no significant structural elaboration effect
핵심: 감정 정교화(emotional elaboration)만으로는 부족 — 구조적 정교화(structural elaboration)가 반드시 함께 필요

Three-Way Interaction: Emotional × Attachment Avoidance × Attachment Anxiety
(β=0.33, p<.01; R²=.44, p<.001 — explains 44% variance)
- FEARFUL-AVOIDANT mothers (high avoidance + high anxiety) = most problematic pattern
- High attachment anxiety: emotional elaboration NEGATIVELY predicts memory specificity (b=−2.96, p<.05)
- Significant specifically for STRESSFUL events (β=.45, Bonferroni p=.016, R²=.35) — not happy/conflict/separation

=== MECHANISM ===

"Emotional elaboration without sufficient structural elaboration, particularly for negative events, may negatively predict memory specificity, as raising emotional content without explanation of its causes or fluent discussion of resolution may activate children's emotional arousal without the maternal support needed to understand the emotional experience."

Fearful-avoidant attachment → cannot simultaneously enter child's emotional experience (elaboration) AND maintain calm, coherent frame (structural support) → dysregulation prevents "serve-and-return" responsiveness.

=== INTERVENTION IMPLICATIONS ===

"Mothers be trained to scaffold emotional exploration with high levels of structural support, as emotional elaboration without such scaffolding may negatively predict child outcomes."

Intervention targets for low-income families:
1. Balanced elaboration: feelings + narrative structure simultaneously
   ("What happened? Why? What did you do? What happened next?")
2. Attachment-informed support: address maternal anxiety/avoidance
3. Event-specific guidance: stressful events require most careful scaffolding

Key insight: 학대 경험 부모도 훈련 가능 — fearful-avoidant 패턴은 개입 가능한 타겟.
