Discussion
The preliminary results of our investigation
of meta-emotion are encouraging. The data suggest that meta-emotion is related to parenting
behavior and to child regulatory physiology;
that child regulatory physiology at age 5 predicts emotion down regulation ability at age 8;
and that parenting, meta-emotion, and the

emotion regulation and one from the metaemotion variables through parenting.
