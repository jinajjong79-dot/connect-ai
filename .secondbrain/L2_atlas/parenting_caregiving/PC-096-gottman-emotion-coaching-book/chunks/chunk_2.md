=== PRACTICAL TOOLS IN THE BOOK ===

1. Parenting Style Self-Assessment (helps parents identify their default approach)
2. Emotion Coaching "Do and Don't" lists
3. Age-appropriate emotion vocabulary lists
4. Role-playing scripts for common challenging situations
5. A guide for fathers specifically (Gottman emphasizes fathers' unique role)

=== THE ROLE OF FATHERS ===

Gottman devotes specific attention to fathers' emotion coaching role:
- Fathers who engage in emotion coaching provide unique benefits
- Father-child rough-and-tumble play teaches emotional regulation in physical contexts
- Fathers who accept children's emotions (especially sons') create emotionally healthy boys
- "Father hunger" (emotional absence of father) negatively impacts emotional development
- A father's involvement in emotion coaching predicts better outcomes than mother's alone
