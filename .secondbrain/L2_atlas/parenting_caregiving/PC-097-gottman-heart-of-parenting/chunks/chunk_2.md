The research program identified the following as highest-leverage interventions:
1. Increase parental awareness of their own emotional history (meta-emotion)
2. Help parents develop comfort with negative emotions (especially anger and sadness)
3. Teach parents to slow down during emotional moments (avoid immediate fixing)
4. Build parents' emotion vocabulary (so they can help children label emotions)
5. Practice empathic responding before problem-solving

=== THE HEART OF PARENTING: KEY INSIGHT ===

The central insight from Gottman's research program, stated most directly:

"The quality of parent-child emotional interactions — specifically, whether parents acknowledge, validate, and coach children through emotional experiences — is the primary determinant of children's emotional intelligence. This matters more than discipline style, academic enrichment, or any specific parenting technique."

The research showed that emotion coaching is not a parenting technique but a philosophy — a way of viewing emotions, children, and the parent-child relationship that produces fundamentally different outcomes.
