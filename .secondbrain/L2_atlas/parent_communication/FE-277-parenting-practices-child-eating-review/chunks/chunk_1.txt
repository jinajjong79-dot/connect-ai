Costa & Oliveira (2023). Healthcare (Basel). PMC9914567. Narrative review (not original empirical study).

Key synthesized findings:

Coercive/Controlling practices:
- Restrictive feeding → food responsiveness↑ (과식 경향)
- Pressure to eat → food fussiness↑ + food enjoyment↓ (편식 악화)
- Instrumental feeding (food as reward, e.g., "eat your vegetables, then you get ice cream") → eating absent hunger cues↑ (배고프지 않아도 먹음 → 과식·편식)

Structure-based practices (positive):
- Structured family mealtimes → self-regulation in eating↑
- Monitoring consumption → overeating↓
- Parental modeling of healthy eating → food fussiness↓

Autonomy-supportive practices (positive):
- Child involvement in food preparation → food fussiness↓
- Nutrition education → food enjoyment↑

Bidirectional effects (longitudinal evidence):
- Child fussy eating → parent pressure to eat↑ → child food avoidance↑ (강압 순환)
- Parent practices ↔ child eating = reciprocal, not unidirectional

Key concept: "음식 보상(도구적 수유)→배고픔 무관 섭취↑·편식 예측; 구조+자율성 지지=보호; 강압 수유는 편식 순환 악화. '잘 먹으면 아이스크림'이 오히려 편식 만든다"
