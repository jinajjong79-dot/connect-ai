# Parental Influence on Children's Media Use in South Korea: National Population-Based Study
## Conclusions

1. **Temporal specificity:** Distinguish daytime from nighttime use reveals differential parental influence patterns.
2. **Dynamic effects:** Parenting strategies' effectiveness shifts over time and across developmental stages.
3. **Maternal influence central:** Mothers' media use most consistently predicted children's use.
4. **Policy:** Evidence supports creating targeted guidance accounting for how influences vary across time periods and developmental stages.
