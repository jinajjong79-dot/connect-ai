=== OVERVIEW ===

This study examined spontaneous family narratives during dinnertime conversations in 37 middle-class families with children ages 9-12. Reveals critical differences in how mothers and fathers contribute to shared family storytelling. Key finding: children function as active "theory builders" who test, question, and refine family narrative interpretations.

=== SAMPLE ===

- N=37 two-parent families
- Focal children ages 9-12
- 76% college-educated, 76% Caucasian
- 30 dual-earner, 7 single-earner households
- Recorded 2-3 dinnertime conversations over 2 weeks (20-45 min each)
- 226 total narratives coded
- Inter-rater reliability: 86% agreement, κ=.82

=== KEY FINDINGS ===

Narrative Frequency:
- Average: 1 narrative every ~5 minutes during family dinners
- Recent narratives (M=4.02) >> remote narratives (M=2.08)
- Independent narratives (M=4.16) > shared family narratives (M=1.95)
- Topics: predominantly social activities and peer interactions (not academic/work)

Mother vs. Father Narrative Roles:
- Mothers PROVIDE more information (M=4.43 vs. fathers M=2.50, children M=1.99)
- Mothers NEGATE more (correct inaccuracies, M=0.19 vs. fathers M=0.07)
- Mothers and fathers REQUEST information at similar rates
- Mothers = "kin keepers": constructing and maintaining shared family narratives
- Fathers = information-seekers focused on present-day problem-solving and mastery

Child Associations with Well-being:
- Mothers who confirmed/negated more in REMOTE family narratives → children with fewer INTERNALIZING problems
- Children's own requests/negations in remote narratives → fewer BOTH internalizing AND externalizing problems
- Fathers: information requests in recent INDEPENDENT narratives → children with fewer behavioral problems

=== CHILDREN AS "THEORY BUILDERS" ===

핵심 개념: 아이들은 가족 서사의 수동적 수용자가 아님.
- 자신이 직접 경험하지 않은 사건에 대한 서사에도 적극 참여
- 질문, 정정, 이해 구축 = "theory builders"
- 가족 역사와의 인지적 참여 → 행동 문제로부터 보호적

=== THEORETICAL SIGNIFICANCE ===

Dual Function of Dinnertime Narratives:
1. 어머니: 가족 역사를 통한 정체성 연속성 구축 (emotional/relational)
2. 아버지: 현재 경험을 통한 문제해결·숙달 촉진 (adaptive/mastery)

Identity Bonds Across Time:
- 반복적 가족 서사 → "identity bonds across time" 형성
- 가족 역사, 조상, 가치관과의 연결 → 소속감 → 행동 조절 보호
- 안전한 가족 정체성이 부적응 행동에 대한 완충 제공
